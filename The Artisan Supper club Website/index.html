The idea

More than a five-course meal

Most dinners end when the plates are cleared Ours are built so that the people around the table leave with something more: a new conversation, a kinder week, a reason to come back

I

Connection

Strangers share one table and one menu We host the evening so conversation finds its own way, without forced icebreakers

II

Wellbeing

Between courses, short and gentle talks on social wellbeing, community and mental health An invitation to reflect, never a lecture

III

Craft

Healthy, home-cooked food, plated with professional care and set on a table dressed for the occasion

  1. I

Cooking classes

Cook with your hands
Eat at the table together

Small, hands-on classes in Greater Vancouver, hosted by Shweta You learn the dishes you have seen at our suppers, then sit down and enjoy them together

What we cook

Three dishes to start with

Chaat, stacked and plated

Crisp, tangy, sweet and cool on one plate Learn to balance yogurt, tamarind chutney and crunch, then plate it with care

Momos, folded by hand

Make the dough, shape the pleats, steam them fresh and pair them with two dips

Rose dessert and cake painting

Finish with something beautiful: petals, pistachio and a painted cake to share at the table
